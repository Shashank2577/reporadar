## Repository of the day

**[melgarafael/DeskcommCRM](/repos/melgarafael/DeskcommCRM)** — Open-source AI sales OS — self-hosted CRM with native AI agents + WhatsApp (WAHA). Open alternative to Kommo, Octadesk & Intercom for any business that sells by chat. MCP-ready, multi-tenant, LGPD.
DeskcommCRM provides a self-hosted alternative to proprietary CRMs by integrating AI agents directly into WhatsApp communications. It allows businesses to automate customer service, lead qualification, and sales processes without relying on costly monthly subscriptions or locked features. The system is designed to be hosted entirely on user-controlled servers, ensuring complete data sovereignty. A streamlined deployment process allows for rapid installation on a VPS, complete with the application, WhatsApp integration, and database. It empowers teams to build complex, AI-driven sales pipelines that interact seamlessly with users on their preferred messaging platform.
