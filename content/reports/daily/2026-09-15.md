## Repository of the day

**[debpalash/VoiceStudio](/repos/debpalash/VoiceStudio)** — VoiceStudio is the open-source, fully-local ElevenLabs alternative — voice cloning, voice design, video dubbing, dictation, transcription & audiobook creation in 646 languages.
VoiceStudio is a comprehensive desktop application designed for advanced, local-first audio processing tasks like text-to-speech, speech-to-text, and voice cloning. It fundamentally operates without requiring user accounts, API keys, subscriptions, or external usage meters, ensuring complete data privacy and zero ongoing costs. The software aggregates 16 distinct Text-To-Speech engines and 11 Automatic Speech Recognition engines to support an extensive catalogue of 646 languages. This makes it a highly versatile tool for generating long-form audio content, performing cross-lingual dubbing, and transcribing dictations natively on macOS, Windows, and Linux machines.
