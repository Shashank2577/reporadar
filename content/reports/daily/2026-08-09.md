## Repository of the day

**[TapXWorld/ChinaTextbook](/repos/TapXWorld/ChinaTextbook)** — 所有小初高、大学PDF教材。
This project centralizes and open-sources digital copies of standard Chinese educational textbooks. It aims to eliminate educational poverty by providing free, unrestricted access to materials that are often paywalled or watermarked on other sites. The repository organizes resources by subject and grade level, focusing heavily on mathematics for primary education. It provides direct download links to PDF versions of the textbooks. The initiative is particularly aimed at helping overseas Chinese families maintain access to domestic educational standards.
