## Repository of the day

**[iv-org/invidious](/repos/iv-org/invidious)** — Invidious is an alternative front-end to YouTube
Invidious acts as a privacy-respecting alternative interface to YouTube. It proxies video streams and metadata through independent servers, completely separating users from Google's native tracking scripts and advertising networks. The platform allows users to subscribe to channels, create playlists, and receive notifications without needing a Google account. By serving video content cleanly and directly, Invidious provides a lightweight, ad-free viewing experience while preserving user anonymity and minimizing bandwidth consumption.
