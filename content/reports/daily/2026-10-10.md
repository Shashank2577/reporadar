## Repository of the day

**[storytold/artcraft](/repos/storytold/artcraft)** — ArtCraft is an intentional crafting engine for artists, designers, and filmmakers
ArtCraft functions as a comprehensive integrated development environment designed specifically for artists working with AI models. It moves beyond simple text prompting by offering visual tools for precise, repeatable results. Users can compose scenes in 2D, stage complex environments in 3D, and integrate multiple assets like backdrops, foregrounds, and props. The platform supports a vast catalog of over 60 models across image, video, music, and 3D generation, enabling a highly controlled and iterative creative workflow.

- Typical use cases: Plan consistent cinematic shots; Build complex layered scenes; Control character poses
