## Repository of the day

**[Graphify-Labs/graphify](/repos/Graphify-Labs/graphify)** — Turn any codebase, with its docs, SQL schemas, configs, and PDFs, into a queryable knowledge graph. A /graphify skill for Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, and Gemini CLI: local deterministic AST parsing, every edge explained, no vector store.
Graphify extracts entities, relationships, and complex semantic meaning from large volumes of unstructured document text. It utilizes language models to identify obscure connections that rigid regex parsers typically miss. The engine structures this extracted data into standardized node and edge formats suitable for direct ingestion by graph databases. It handles the entity resolution process to merge duplicate references into single, highly connected conceptual nodes. By automating the extraction pipeline, it allows organizations to map complex internal knowledge networks efficiently.
