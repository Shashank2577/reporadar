## Repository of the day

**[earthtojake/text-to-cad](/repos/earthtojake/text-to-cad)** — Give your agent CAD superpowers.
Text-to-CAD is an innovative application that allows users to generate Computer-Aided Design (CAD) models simply by describing the desired object using natural language text prompts. It leverages Large Language Models to interpret the text and construct the 3D geometry programmatically, typically outputting code that defines the shape. This approach bridges the gap between conceptualization and 3D modeling, enabling rapid prototyping without requiring extensive knowledge of complex, graphical CAD software interfaces. It exports generated models into standardized formats like STEP, STL, and GLB, facilitating downstream fabrication and simulation.
