## Repository of the day

**[permissionlesstech/bitchat](/repos/permissionlesstech/bitchat)** — bluetooth mesh chat, IRC vibes
Bitchat is a decentralized peer-to-peer messaging application that utilizes a dual transport architecture, enabling local communication via Bluetooth mesh networks and global messaging through the Nostr protocol. It prioritizes user privacy by eliminating the need for accounts, phone numbers, or central servers.

This application provides a solution for secure and private communication, especially in scenarios where internet access is limited or unavailable. Its decentralized nature helps protect user data from centralized control and potential censorship.
- Stars: 30,876 | Forks: 4,827 | Language: Swift | License: Unlicense
- Typical use cases: Offline communication in disaster scenarios; Private group chats without centralized servers; Location-based messaging in specific geographic areas
