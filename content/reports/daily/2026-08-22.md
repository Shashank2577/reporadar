## Repository of the day

**[mahlernim/google-timeline-visualizer](/repos/mahlernim/google-timeline-visualizer)** — Visualize your year in travel using your Google Location History (Timeline) data
Timeline Visualizer takes an exported Google Maps Timeline JSON file and transforms it into an animated, map-based MP4 video on an Android device or via a web interface. It processes the location history locally, applying filters to remove anomalous GPS data while rendering the route over CARTO basemaps based on OpenStreetMap. The tool offers customizable settings such as camera tracking behavior, journey duration, and date range selection to tailor the final animation. By processing all data on the client side, it ensures that sensitive location history is never uploaded to a remote server.
- Stars: 2,331 | Forks: 271 | Language: Kotlin | License: MIT
- Typical use cases: Travel memory preservation; Social media sharing; Route visualization
