## Repository of the day

**[bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view](/repos/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view)** — A spy satellite simulator in your browser, except the data is real. Live open source spatial intelligence on a photorealistic 3D globe.
God's Eye View is an interactive, browser-based spatial intelligence simulator that aggregates real-time open-source data onto a photorealistic 3D globe. It visualizes live feeds of aircraft, ships, satellites, earthquakes, and traffic alongside public camera streams. When live video feeds are unavailable, the system intelligently falls back to clearly labeled modeled views to maintain continuous situational awareness. The interface provides hands-free operation through voice control powered by a real-time AI agent. By transforming abstract intelligence signals into a cohesive virtual place, it fundamentally changes how users interact with global data.
