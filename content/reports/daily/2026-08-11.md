## Repository of the day

**[paperclipai/paperclip](/repos/paperclipai/paperclip)** — The open-source app everyone uses to manage agents at work
Paperclip provides a management interface for deploying and coordinating AI agents across various professional tasks. It allows users to define workflows, assign specific roles to agents, and monitor their execution in real time. The platform focuses on orchestrating multiple agents to complete multi-step business processes efficiently. By serving as a central hub, it simplifies the lifecycle management of automated workflows. It aims to bridge the gap between individual AI capabilities and complex enterprise automation needs.
