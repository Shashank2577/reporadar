## Repository of the day

**[zhouxiaoka/autoclip](/repos/zhouxiaoka/autoclip)** — AutoClip｜The open-source OpusClip alternative — local-first AI clipping & MCP server, zero subscriptions. 一个链接，一键出片。开源 AI 视频剪辑桌面工具，将播客、访谈、课程等长视频自动剪成短视频，生成字幕、封面和发布文案，适配抖音、小红书、TikTok、Reels 与 YouTube Shorts。
AutoClip is an automated video processing system designed to download videos from platforms like YouTube and Bilibili and intelligently extract highlight clips. It utilizes large language models, specifically Qwen, to analyze the video content and identify the most engaging segments. The system automatically cuts the video into smaller clips and can organize them into curated collections. It features a modern React-based frontend and a robust FastAPI backend supported by a Celery task queue for handling asynchronous processing. This pipeline dramatically reduces the manual effort required for secondary video creation and social media clipping.
