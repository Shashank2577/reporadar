gstack provides a highly opinionated, pre-configured architectural template that drastically reduces the friction of starting new web projects. It wires together modern frontend frameworks with lightweight backend services, establishing secure database connections and authentication flows out of the box. The project enforces strict directory structures and styling conventions to eliminate decision fatigue during the critical early stages of development.
