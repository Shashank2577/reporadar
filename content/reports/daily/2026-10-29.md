## Repository of the day

**[HKUDS/DeepTutor](/repos/HKUDS/DeepTutor)** — DeepTutor: Lifelong Personalized Tutoring. https://deeptutor.info/.
DeepTutor is a comprehensive AI-powered educational platform designed to act as a personal tutor and learning assistant. It integrates tightly with external knowledge bases, document parsers, and web search providers to ground its educational content in specific, user-provided materials. The system features an immersive reading environment where source documents are displayed alongside the chat, allowing the AI to cite pages directly. It can autonomously generate structured books, quizzes, and learning mastery paths from raw documents or topics. With support for numerous LLM providers, multi-agent collaboration, and a robust Model Context Protocol (MCP) tool ecosystem, it provides a highly customizable learning experience accessible via web UI, CLI, and various messaging platforms.
