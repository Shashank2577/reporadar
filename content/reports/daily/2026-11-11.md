## Repository of the day

**[ZhuLinsen/daily_stock_analysis](/repos/ZhuLinsen/daily_stock_analysis)** — LLM 驱动的多市场股票智能分析系统：多源行情、实时新闻、决策看板与自动推送，支持零成本定时运行。  LLM-powered multi-market stock analysis system with multi-source market data, real-time news, decision dashboard, automated notifications, and cost-free scheduled runs.
Daily Stock Analysis is a Python-based tool designed to automatically fetch and process daily trading data for the Chinese A-share market. It aggregates historical price data, volume metrics, and fundamental indicators from various financial data sources. The system then applies technical analysis algorithms to identify trends, calculate moving averages, and flag potential trading signals based on predefined strategies. By outputting the results into structured formats and visual charts, it allows investors to quickly digest market movements without manual data collection. The project serves as a foundational pipeline for quantitative analysis and algorithmic trading research.
