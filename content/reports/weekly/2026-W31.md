## Repository of the week

**[permissionlesstech/bitchat](/repos/permissionlesstech/bitchat)** — bluetooth mesh chat, IRC vibes
BitChat enables peer-to-peer messaging over Bluetooth mesh networks for offline communication and the Nostr protocol for global connectivity. It eliminates the need for accounts, phone numbers, or central servers, ensuring privacy and resilience. Its hybrid architecture intelligently selects between local mesh and internet relays for message delivery.
